# Design System: Joymify

## Overview

Joymify is a component library for task-focused apps (register: **product**); the landing suite (`HeroSection`, `CTASection`, `PricingCard`, `LandingPage` and friends) is designed in the **brand** register. Personality: **composed, exact, warm**. Dense where data demands it, calm everywhere else. Gold is a signature, not a wash. Product context lives in `PRODUCT.md`.

**Token contract.** Components read the semantic layer (`--joy-surface-*`, `--joy-text-*`, `--joy-border-*`, `--joy-accent-*`, `--joy-status-*`, `--joy-focus-ring`, `--joy-elevation-*`). Primitive ramps (`--joy-color-*`) only feed that layer. Colour literals live only in `packages/tokens/src/tokens.json`. Status colours (`--joy-status-*`: green, amber, red, blue) are strictly reserved for semantic state and feedback, keeping them distinct from brand accents and general theme surfaces.

**Theme switching.** `data-theme="dark"` or `data-theme="light"` on `<html>` wins. Without it, `prefers-color-scheme` picks the theme. Both themes are designed: dark is not inverted light.

### Mobile rules

- 375px is the first viewport: no horizontal scroll, grids collapse to one column, tables stack or scroll inside their own container.
- Touch targets are at least 44 x 44 CSS px on coarse pointers (buttons, inputs, checkboxes, tabs, pagination, close buttons).
- Side gutters are at least 16px. Input text is at least 16px so iOS does not zoom.
- Select-family overlays become a bottom sheet on small screens; hover-only affordances have a touch alternative.

## Colors

Four brand anchors: navy `#14253e` (primary surface anchor), gold `#c49a3a` (the single accent), cream `#f8f3e8` (warm canvas), ink `#1f1c13` (primary text). Neutrals are tinted toward the brand, not slate.

Semantic status colors (`success` green, `warning` amber, `danger` red, `info` blue) are preserved exclusively for feedback, state communication, and validation. They must never serve as decorative styling, general section backgrounds, or primary brand accents.

## Brand

The mark is a gold diamond on a navy tile, not a square and not a circle: outline `M16 4 28 16 16 28 4 16Z`, inner fill `M16 9 23 16 16 23 9 16Z`, a centre dot, on a 32×32 tile with `rx=6`, defined once in `packages/brand/src/geometry.js`. The wordmark is "Joymify" in sentence case, set in the display font and converted to vector outlines (no live text, no font dependency at render time). The canonical mark : gap : glyph ratio is `48 : 16 : 40`. Components render the wordmark at padding 0, so the mark height equals the `height` prop and the glyph is drawn with `overflow: visible`, deliberately overflowing its box by about 8% of height on each edge. Parents must not clip the `Wordmark` with `overflow: hidden` unless they add matching padding back. Downloadable wordmark assets (`packages/brand/assets/`) bake in 16px padding at 80px height.

Display font: `--joy-font-family-display` (`"Cormorant Garamond", "Noto Serif", Georgia, serif`). joy-design does not load the font; consumers do.

Colour roles: `--joy-brand-tile`, `--joy-brand-mark`, `--joy-brand-mark-on-tile`, `--joy-brand-mark-contrast`, `--joy-brand-ink`, `--joy-brand-ink-on-tile` — light and dark values are in the [Semantic layer](#semantic-layer) table above.

### Don'ts

- Do not recolour the mark or wordmark outside the `--joy-brand-*` roles above.
- Do not stretch or skew the mark or wordmark; scale it uniformly.
- Do not substitute the square mark some products still ship; the diamond is canonical.
- Gold is a signature, not a wash: never a large field, a page background or a card background. The two sanctioned small-area gold fills are the mark's inner diamond and the `Ribbon` `gold` tone.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- Use semantic tokens for every colour, radius, space, shadow, duration and z-index.
- Design every interactive component for default, hover, focus-visible, active, disabled, loading, error and selected where they apply, at every size it ships.
- Check both themes, at 375px and 1280px, with contrast computed, before calling UI work done.
- Keep Svelte and React twins identical in props, classes and rendering.
- Spend boldness once per surface (the hero CTA, the hub canvas); everything else recedes.
- Carry status with icon plus text, never colour alone.
- Strictly reserve semantic status colors: red for danger/error, amber for warning, green for success, blue for info.

### Don't:

- Hex, `rgb()` or `hsl()` literals outside `packages/tokens/src/tokens.json`, or `var(--joy-x, fallback)` fallbacks (`scripts/lint-colors.sh` fails the build).
- Reference primitive ramps (`--joy-color-navy-500` and friends) from a component; use the semantic role.
- Tailwind-default SaaS colours: purple primaries, slate neutrals, stock blue links, stock status greens and reds.
- Inverted light mode: white cards on a dark canvas, grey shadows on dark, pure white body text.
- Side-stripe `border-left` accents wider than 1px, gradient text, decorative glassmorphism.
- Identical icon + heading + text card grids, a tracked all-caps eyebrow above every section, 01/02/03 markers on content that is not a sequence.
- An arrow glyph appended to link text, `#111` standing in for black, middle-dot meta strings.
- Raw OS emoji anywhere; use the SVG icons in `@montionugera/joy-icons`.
- A flat single-colour hero button with a colour-only hover, a spinner-only loading state, or 32px controls on a phone.
- Use semantic status colors (green, amber, red) for arbitrary decorative styling, general section backgrounds, or brand theme accents.
