Joy combined theme

Rules

Gold

Use for. The mark. Something live: the Live pill, the lit voice level, today’s column, a running job. The one main action of a screen, as a fill, in both modes.

Never. Text, a fill larger than a button, a card background, a destructive action such as End call, or a stand-in for amber. More than one gold button on a screen.

Gradient

Use for. First Light is the call screen background and brand moments such as sign-in or launch. One per screen, same six stops in both modes, mixed in oklab; navy meets ember at a dark neutral stop, never through plum.

Never. Cards, buttons, text, charts or any data screen. Words and the mark over it stay above the ember stop; everything else sits on the solid deck.

Seams

Use for. The join between two sections of one surface: masthead and body, list and side column, one panel and the next. A diamond drawn out to the width of the join, 6px at the middle, in two flat gold facets.

Never. Card outlines, control borders, row or table-header rules, or a stripe down the side of an item. Those use the cool hairline or nothing. Never more than one seam per join.

Status colours

Use for. Green, amber, red and blue for state only, each with its icon and a word.

Never. Decoration, chart series, links, emphasis, or buttons.

Diamond

Use for. One unit of something counted: a check-in, a level step. The mark, and the lit mark of a live thing.

Never. A status icon, a selection tick, a container for text, or a pattern.
