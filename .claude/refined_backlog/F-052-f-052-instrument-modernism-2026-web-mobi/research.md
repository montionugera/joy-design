# F-052: Instrument Modernism — 2026 Web/Mobile Responsiveness, Joy-Coach Suite, and Interactive Demos — research notes

## Prior Art & Industry Benchmarks
1. **Linear / Raycast**: The 2026 standard for calm, high-efficiency infrastructure. 1px directional ambient highlight, tactile sunken wells, keyboard-first navigation (`Cmd+K`).
2. **Whoop / SwingVision / Strava**: The Three-Second Glanceability Test for sports telemetry. Progressive disclosure (top-level status → expandable deep-dive drawer).
3. **2026 Thumb-Zone Ergonomics**: 75% of mobile interactions are thumb-driven one-handed in the bottom 35% safe zone. Touch target floor of 48px to 56px for athletic mobile use.
4. **Outdoor Sunlight Legibility**: 50,000 lux noon glare requires WCAG 2.2 AAA contrast (≥7.0:1), 2px solid borders, and solid pill badges instead of 10% translucent tints.
