Joy Design theme candidates

Four directions for a modern Joy Design, each shown on the same small call screen in light and dark mode, with the common charcoal look last for comparison. Every direction keeps the diamond mark and reserves green, amber, red and blue for status.

Judge them with the name covered: which one could only be Joy?

Suggested pick: Midnight Brass as the everyday base, with the First Light gradient as the one signature surface (call screen, launch screen, brand pages). They share the navy, so they combine without a second palette.

Midnight BrassFirst LightKintsugiVelvetCharcoal and off-white

Candidate A Restrained

Midnight Brass

Today’s navy and gold, tightened. Cream is swapped for a cool porcelain tinted toward the navy, corners go sharp, and gold becomes a brass sheen used only where something is live.

Gradient: Brass sheen on the mark and live states only; a faint navy glow behind dark screens. Corners: 3px.

Candidate B Committed, gradient-led

First Light

The minute before sunrise: night navy lifting through dusk violet into copper and gold. The gradient is the signature surface for the call screen and brand moments; everything else stays flat.

Gradient: Full horizon gradient as the screen background; the same warm end as the live colour. Corners: 14px.

Candidate C Restrained, light-first

Kintsugi

Porcelain surfaces joined by thin gold seams. Gold never fills anything: it only draws lines, so the brand colour reads as craft instead of decoration.

Gradient: Gold only as hairline seams that fade at both ends; a soft glaze highlight on cards. Corners: 10px.

Candidate D Departure, bold

Velvet

Navy deepens into aubergine, and gold softens into champagne and rose-gold. Plush, evening, clearly not a bank. The furthest from today’s brand.

Gradient: Navy to plum across dark screens; rose-gold to champagne for live states. Corners: 18px.
