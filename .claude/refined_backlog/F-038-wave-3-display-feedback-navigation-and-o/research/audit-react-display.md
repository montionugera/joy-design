## Summary
Files audited: 39 React components and modules in packages/react/src (Alert, Anchor, Avatar, AvatarGroup, Badge, Breadcrumb, Divider, Drawer, Dropdown, EmptyState, Flex, ImagePreview [with Image], MarkdownViewer, Menu, Message, MetricTile, Modal, Notification, Pagination, Popconfirm, Progress, Result, Skeleton, Space, Spin, Splitter, StatCard, Statistic, Steps, Table, Tabs, Tag, Timeline, Toast, Tooltip, BarChart, LineChart, CandlestickChart, chart-core.ts), corresponding CSS in packages/tokens/build.js, and twin implementations in packages/svelte/src/lib.
Issue count by severity: 105 issues total (P0: 24 blocks production / P1: 67 visibly sloppy / P2: 14 polish).
Verdict: Fails production grade in the product register due to severe dark-mode contrast failures (chart HUD labels at 1.16:1, axis labels at 2.56:1, missing alert/badge dark themes), rampant foreign purple (#7c3aed) and cool-slate (#1e293b, #0f172a) palette pollution, missing React.forwardRef and rest-prop drops across 19 components, lack of mobile touch target dimensions (<44px), unstyled classes in ImagePreview and EmptyState, and violations of absolute AI-slop bans (glassmorphism blur, side-stripe border-left > 1px, tracked uppercase eyebrows).

## Systemic patterns
- **Severe dark-mode contrast collapse in charts & HUD**: BarChart, LineChart, and CandlestickChart hardcode `style={{ color: 'var(--navy-500, #14253e)' }}` for HUD labels, which against the dark canvas `#0d1829` collapses to a completely illegible 1.16:1 contrast ratio.
- **Widespread foreign purple (#7c3aed) and Tailwind cool-slate pollution**: Rather than tokenized brand anchors (`--navy-500: #14253e`, `--gold-400: #c49a3a`, `--ink-500: #1f1c13`), components and `build.js` inject purple (`#7c3aed`, `#ede9fe`, `#c084fc`, `#a855f7`) across global `:focus-visible`, Tabs active state, Pagination, Menu, Tag, Spin, Splitter, Steps, Timeline, and fallbacks in Message/Notification. Dark mode rules hardcode cool-slate (`#0f172a`, `#1e293b`, `#334155`, `#cbd5e1`).
- **Pervasive omission of dark mode overrides**: Alert, Badge, Tabs, Dropdown items, and Tooltip have zero `[data-theme="dark"]` rules in `build.js`, leaving light pastels (`#eff6ff`, `#ede9fe`, `#f5f3ff`) and un-adapted borders glowing starkly on dark surfaces.
- **Systemic ref and rest-prop swallowing in React (19 components)**: Alert, AvatarGroup, Badge, Breadcrumb, Drawer, Dropdown, EmptyState, ImagePreview, MarkdownViewer, MetricTile, Modal, Notification, Pagination, Popconfirm, Progress, Skeleton, StatCard, Table, Tabs, Toast, and Tooltip fail to wrap in `React.forwardRef` and/or swallow rest props, breaking DOM ref measurement, test IDs, and ARIA attributes.
- **Mobile touch target failures (<44px)**: Pagination buttons (32x32px), Tab buttons (~35px), Menu items (~30px), Breadcrumb links (~14px), Tag close buttons (~12x12px), ImagePreview toolbar buttons (36x36px), Splitter drag bars (6px), and Modal/Drawer/Toast close buttons (~18-20px) all fail the mandatory WCAG/mobile 44x44px touch target requirement.
- **Missing CSS rule coverage & inline-style crutches**: `ImagePreview.tsx` relies completely on 11+ inline style objects while classes `joy-image-preview-mask`, `joy-image-preview-toolbar`, and `joy-image-wrapper` have zero rules in `build.js`; `EmptyState.tsx` was left unstyled outside Svelte-scoped CSS.
- **Direct violations of absolute AI-slop bans**: Side-stripe `border-left > 1px` accents in Alert, Toast, and MarkdownViewer blockquotes; tracked ALL-CAPS text in MetricTile and StatCard labels; and decorative glassmorphism (`backdrop-filter: blur`) in Drawer, Spin, Chart HUD, and ImagePreview.
- **Unverified, low-contrast canvas text in chart engine**: `chart-core.ts` hardcodes axis text fills to `#94a3b8` on light canvas (2.56:1 contrast) and `#64748b` on dark canvas (3.74:1 contrast), both failing WCAG 2.2 AA (4.5:1).
- **Overlay focus traps and keyboard operability absent**: Modal and Drawer do not trap keyboard focus or lock background body scrolling; Dropdown and Popconfirm lack outside-click dismissal and Escape listeners; Tabs and Splitter lack keyboard arrow-key navigation.
- **Framework parity drift against Svelte twins**: Callback casing mismatches (`onChange` vs `onchange`, `onSort` vs `onsort`), missing two-way binding support (`$bindable`), divergence in file structure (`Image` bundled in `ImagePreview.tsx` vs standalone `Image.svelte`), and differences in prop types.
