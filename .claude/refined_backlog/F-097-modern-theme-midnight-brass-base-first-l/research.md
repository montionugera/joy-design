# Modern theme for Joy Design: research and specimen

- **Idea:** I-108
- **Status:** Proposed. A specimen exists; nothing in `packages/tokens` has changed.
- **Date:** 2026-11-23
- **Decider:** pasitnusso

## What the owner chose

Four colour directions were shown side by side (`specimen/candidates-a-b-c-d.html`, generated by `specimen/candidates-gen.py`). The owner picked three and they are merged into one system, each with one role:

- **A, Midnight Brass:** the everyday base. Cool porcelain and navy surfaces instead of cream; gold only for live states and the one main action.
- **B, First Light:** the one signature gradient (night navy, dusk violet, copper, gold), for the call screen and brand moments only.
- **C, Kintsugi:** the detail motif. Thin gold hairline seams that fade at both ends, at joins.

Dropped: **D, Velvet** (aubergine and champagne; too close to the "purple primary" anti-reference in `PRODUCT.md`). Charcoal and off-white was shown only as the common look to avoid.

## The combined specimen

`specimen/joy-combined-theme.html` shows the merged theme on a call screen and a data-heavy week overview, each in light and dark. It is generated by `specimen/joy-combined-build.py` (edit that, not the HTML). `specimen/joy-combined-contrast.py` computes every text pair it covers and exits non-zero on a failure; it passes.

How it was made: two builders took different stances ("Instrument": sharp, gold main button; "Glaze": softer, porcelain button, structural seams), each was reviewed independently from real phone and desktop screenshots, one synthesizer merged them, and a fresh reviewer checked the result.

## Independent review of the final page

- Clean technical pass: **True**
- Banned patterns found: **none**
- Reads as one system: **True**
- Distinctiveness: **6 of 10** (5 means competent but anonymous). Both earlier variants also scored 6.
- Strongest: The diamond as the counting unit. It is the progress steps in the table, the stacked check-ins in the chart, the voice level, the Live and Running indicators and the concentric mark on both the masthead and the call screen. It carries data and brand at once, and it is what makes the two screens read as one system, more than the palette does. Second: discipline holds. Status colours appear only with an icon and a word, gold appears only on the mark, live things and the single Start call button, and no text sits on the warm end of the gradient (the script proves the geometry). The phone table reflow (name and status on line one, progress and kind on line two) is clean at 390px in both modes.
- Weakest: The two things meant to be signature are the least convincing. The gold seam is close to invisible: in light mode it reads as a grey-brown hairline, so with the logo hidden nobody would name 'gold seams' as a motif. The sunrise gradient is a navy-to-purple-to-orange dusk, which is familiar from many wellness and voice apps; only the diamond mark on top of it makes it this brand. Take the diamonds away and the data screen is a competent, anonymous navy admin page in the system font. 'Composed' and 'exact' come through; 'warm' lives almost entirely in the call screen.

## Open questions for the owner

- Dark amber: joy-design's dark warning-fg #DFAE68 is 5 RGB units from brass gold #DAB362. The page uses First Light's copper #E08A4E (6.5:1 on the dark card) for dark warnings instead. Accept this as the new dark warning-fg in joy-design, or keep the token and move something else?
- Gold main action in light mode: theme A's light palette has a navy main button. The page makes the main action gold in both modes and puts it on a navy masthead. Keep, or go back to navy in light?
- Navy masthead on the light data screen: this is the main fix for 'light data screen looks anonymous'. Is a dark header band acceptable in light mode across apps?
- Light call screen keeps the night-to-dawn sky instead of a pale version (the pale one drifts into the banned cream). Is a dark call screen in light mode acceptable?
- End call is a neutral solid button, not gold and not red (red is reserved for status). Should a destructive action be allowed to use the danger red?
- Light status text uses the 600 step of the status ramp (#306D35, #865000, #9F3331, #2C6193) because the 700 step that tokens.json maps to status fg reads near-black at 14px. Change the semantic mapping in joy-design?
- Radii settle at 3px controls and 4px cards (theme A). Glaze proposed 8/12. Confirm sharp corners system-wide.
- The page uses the system font only, as required. Both reviewers said the typeface adds no identity; a brand typeface is a separate decision.

## Before this becomes a spec

- The distinctiveness score did not rise above 6 in any of three reviews. The diamond works as the shape language; the gold seam and the gradient, which were meant to be the signatures, are the least convincing parts. That needs a design answer, not a polish pass.
- The typeface is untouched (system font in every specimen). `Cormorant Garamond`, `Plus Jakarta Sans` and `Inter` are all very widely used; the typeface is a separate decision and probably the larger lever.
- The colours are hex proposals. They have not been run through the token ramps in `packages/tokens` or through `packages/tokens/test/contrast.test.js`.
- The Flutter, Swift, Svelte and React kits read generated tokens, so a token change reaches all of them; component-level changes (sharp radii, the diamond marks, seams) do not come for free.

### The gradient needs the owner's eye

A sky that goes from navy to copper has to cross the colour wheel somewhere, and there are only three ways to do it. Each version of the specimen took one:

1. **Plum middle** (first version): the classic sunrise; the reviewer called the middle "purple-mauve, the least on-brand colour on the page".
2. **Smoky middle** (second version): no purple, but a grey band about 30px deep where navy meets copper.
3. **Dark middle** (third version, the one in `specimen/` now): the crossing happens while the sky is still dark, so nothing reads purple or grey. The cost, in the checker's words: the upper 55 to 60 percent is "a dark slab, not a lifting sky", copper arrives only in the bottom third, and in dark mode the bright bottom edge meets the dark deck with a hard line.

Three attempts is the limit for tuning by guesswork, so this stops here. The choice between the three is taste, and it is the owner's. Stops for version 3: `#0B1226, #101B38, #2A2014, #4A2A14, #C7743F, #F2C078`, mixed in oklab. Version 2: `#0B1226, #15264A, #2A2B33, #63381A, #C7743F, #F2C078`. Version 1: theme B's five stops, `#0B1226, #1F2352, #6B3A5B, #C7774A, #F0BE78`.

Still open from the checker (none high): the outer rings of the mark are faint where they cross the warm band; the solid light button for End call is the brightest thing on the dark deck; the data screen's warmth rests on gold alone (pure white cards against the brief's "warm greys").
